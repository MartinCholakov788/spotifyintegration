THIRD-PARTY FONTS in Spotify Integration Mod
==============================================

The extra fonts of the player (Settings > Fonts) are small subsets (only the letters, numbers and punctuation the player
needs) of free fonts. They were renamed inside the files ("Zlorbie ...") as their licences ask for modified copies. The mod's own
code is MIT licensed (see LICENSE); these fonts keep their own licences, below.
